## AquariOS Launches the World's First Whole-Being AI Operating System on WhatsApp, Powered by Anthropic's Claude

*Brazilian health-tech startup introduces the iVi 4D — a single AI-derived vitality score combining Physical, Mental, Spiritual, and Social dimensions — available to 2 billion WhatsApp users across 13 countries*

---

**BELO HORIZONTE, Brazil — September 9, 2026** — AquariOS, a Brazilian health-tech company, today announced the global launch of its whole-being personal AI platform across six countries simultaneously, with expansion to thirteen countries planned through 2027. Built on Anthropic's Claude and distributed natively through Meta's WhatsApp Business API, AquariOS introduces ProteOS — a conversational AI well-being agent that operates entirely within WhatsApp, requiring no additional app download.

At the core of the platform is the **iVi 4D** (Índice de Vitalidade Integral), the first quantitative well-being index to combine four human dimensions in a single AI-derived score: Physical (35%), Mental (30%), Spiritual (20%), and Social (15%). Unlike fitness trackers that measure one dimension of human health, iVi 4D gives users a unified picture of where they stand — and where to act.

"I spent 36 years treating patients. I watched people take extraordinary care of everything around them while slowly disappearing from themselves," said **Fabiano Gomes Leite**, founder and CEO of AquariOS. "After my own health crisis, I understood that what was missing wasn't another app — it was a system. A personal operating system for the whole human being. That is AquariOS."

### Powered by Anthropic. Distributed by Meta.

ProteOS, the AI agent embedded in AquariOS, is powered by **Anthropic's Claude** — one of the world's most capable and safety-focused AI models. The choice of Claude was deliberate: AquariOS operates in the intersection of AI and human well-being, where model safety, nuance, and the ability to hold complex human context are non-negotiable.

"We chose Anthropic because ProteOS needs to understand the whole person, not just answer questions," said Leite. "Claude gives us the depth and safety guarantees that health-adjacent AI requires."

Distribution runs through **Meta's WhatsApp Business API**, positioning AquariOS where its users already live: WhatsApp is the primary communication infrastructure for four of the six Wave 1 launch countries — Brazil, Nigeria, Peru, and Venezuela. With over 2 billion monthly active users globally, WhatsApp enables AquariOS to reach populations where traditional app-store distribution fails.

### Global from Day One: 13 Countries, 10 Languages

AquariOS launches in Wave 1 with six countries: Brazil, United States, Portugal, Nigeria, Peru, and Venezuela. The platform supports Portuguese (Brazilian and European), English, and Spanish, with Norwegian, German, Korean, Thai, Traditional Chinese, Hebrew, and Farsi planned across subsequent waves.

The iVi 4D score is calculated in real time and adapts its language, cultural framing, and the six ProteOS response archetypes (called Bardos) to the user's country and emotional state — a level of cultural personalization unprecedented in the well-being category.

### Architecture: Purpose-Built for Privacy

AquariOS was designed with a zero-trust, multi-layer security architecture. User health data is never sold or shared. The platform complies with Brazil's LGPD, the EU's GDPR, and equivalent frameworks across all 13 countries. CerberOS, the platform's internal compliance and safety layer, audits every AI response before delivery.

### Availability and Pricing

AquariOS is available immediately as an Android application (iOS pending), and via WhatsApp in Wave 1 countries. Pricing tiers include a permanent free plan and Starter, Premium, and Professional subscriptions. Nigeria and Venezuela are served on the free tier at launch, reflecting the company's commitment to accessibility in markets where traditional subscription pricing creates barriers.
