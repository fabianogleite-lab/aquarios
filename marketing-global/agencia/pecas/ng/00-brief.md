# /brief ng — Campaign Brief (Wave 1 · Launch 09/09/2026)

**Idioma:** en-NG · **Gateway:** Paystack / NGN · **Onda:** 1

**Contexto:**
Nigeria has 60+ million people affected by mental health conditions — with a treatment gap above 90%. The dominant cultural narrative: "you must be strong." Vulnerability is weakness. Therapy is for "mad people." Yet post-COVID, Gen Z and millennials in Lagos and Abuja are quietly challenging this taboo — especially in the tech and creative communities. Both Christianity and Islam — which together account for nearly all of Nigeria's population — frame the body as a sacred trust ("your body is a temple"). This is the bridge: well-being not as therapy, but as duty, discipline, and performance. WhatsApp is the social infrastructure (65M+ users). AquariOS positions as a *performance OS for whole humans*, not a mental health app.

**Objetivo:** Teaser → 300K impressions, 4K pre-registrations; Launch → 900K impressions, 8% CTR, 15K downloads; Sustain → 30-day retention ≥32%, 2+ Tier-1 features (TechCabal, Nairametrics, Pulse NG).

**Público:** Professionals 26–42 in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt. Tech workers, entrepreneurs, creatives. Often supporting parents and siblings financially. "I carry everyone. Who's checking on me?"

**Mensagem (Big Idea):** "Strength begins within." — Hero persona: Emeka Okafor (32, backend dev at a Lagos fintech, breadwinner for his family of five); has never been "allowed" to not be okay; his iVi score shows what no one else sees.

**Posicionamento CRÍTICO:**
- NEVER call it a "mental health app" — use "well-being OS", "vitality system", "performance tracker"
- Frame wellness as STRENGTH and OPTIMIZATION, not vulnerability or healing
- Faith-adjacent (not preachy): "taking care of what you've been trusted with"

**Canais:** 1º Instagram (Reels/feed) · 2º WhatsApp Status + Communities · 3º TikTok · 4º Twitter/X (Lagos tech Twitter is influential) · 5º LinkedIn (corporate professional angle).

**Formatos prioritários:** feed 1080², Reels 30–45s, WhatsApp image cards.

**Tom:** Empowering, direct, community-aware. No pity. No fragility. Language of builders, performers, and providers. Warmth underneath the strength. "You're not breaking down. You're building up."

**Restrições (gate):**
- No images of sadness, crying, or visible vulnerability
- No therapy/healing/treatment language
- Maintain faith neutrality — if mentioning God/faith, represent both Christianity and Islam equally or use neither
- No stereotyping of Nigerian life (poverty, corruption, struggle) — the audience is aspirational
- Regra #10 integral

**Prazo:** Teaser assets ready T-3 (mid-August 2026).
