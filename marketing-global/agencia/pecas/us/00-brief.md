# /brief us — Campaign Brief (Wave 1 · Launch 09/09/2026)

**Idioma:** en-US · **Gateway:** Stripe / USD · **Onda:** 1

**Contexto:**
The US wellness market exceeds $480B yet 78% of Americans report feeling more fragmented than before the wellness boom. 40 million Americans cannot access mental health care due to cost ($150–200/session). "Spiritual but not religious" accounts for 26% of adults — a massive audience with no well-being home. Apple Health, Whoop, and Calm track data but none address Meaning or Social connection as measurable dimensions. Post-pandemic, purpose and meaning rank as top concerns for adults 28–45 (Pew 2024). This is the gap AquariOS fills: the first whole-being OS — Physical · Mental · Meaning · Social.

**Objetivo:** Teaser → 600K impressions, 8K pre-registrations; Launch → 2M impressions, 9% CTR, 40K downloads; Sustain → 30-day retention ≥40%, 5+ Tier-1 media mentions.

**Público:** Professionals 28–45 in major metros (SF, NYC, Austin, Miami). High achievers, wellness-aware, have tried multiple apps and feel over-tracked/under-understood. $60K+ income. "I optimize everything — why do I still feel lost?"

**Mensagem (Big Idea):** "Whole-being, one number." — Hero persona: Alex Chen (34, PM at a tech company, SF); has 12 health apps; feels more fragmented than ever; his iVi score shows the real picture.

**Positioning (critical):** NOT a mental health app. NOT a meditation app. NOT a biometrics tracker. It's an OS — a layer above all of them that shows you the whole picture. Frame: *the first app to measure what actually makes you human.*

**Canais:** 1st Instagram (Reels/carousel/Stories) · 2nd TikTok · 3rd LinkedIn (B2B angle: workplace wellbeing) · 4th YouTube Pre-roll · 5th Reddit (r/productivity, r/mentalhealth — organic).

**Formatos prioritários:** feed 1080², story 1080×1920, Reels 30–45s.

**Tom:** Direct, intelligent, slightly irreverent. Respects the audience's intelligence. No woo-woo. No empty affirmations. Evidence-adjacent without being clinical. Think: "The Economist meets Calm's aesthetic."

**Restrições (gate):**
- "Spiritual" → always frame as "Meaning" or "Purpose" in US market
- No medical claims — never "diagnose", "treat", "cure"
- No "replacement for therapy" language (FTC/ADA)
- No oversell — US culture penalizes undelivered promises
- Regra #10 integral: no person/story used as negative example

**Prazo:** Teaser assets ready T-3 (mid-August 2026).
