## COMPONENT 2: RESPONSE TEMPLATES

### TEMPLATE A: Minor False Claim (Quick Denial)
**Trigger:** Single account / false allegation / no evidence  
**Response Time:** 30 minutes  
**Platform:** Twitter + internal email  

#### Twitter Response (150 chars)
```
We take privacy seriously. This claim is false. Aquários does NOT track 
location 24/7 and does NOT sell data. We're happy to explain our actual 
security at [privacy@aquarios.com]. #PrivacyMatters
```

#### Internal Email (To Users)
```
Subject: Clarification on Privacy Claims

Dear Aquários Community,

We became aware of false claims about Aquários tracking and data sharing. 
We want to clarify:

✅ We do NOT track your location 24/7
✅ We do NOT sell your data to third parties
✅ We encrypt your diary and health data
✅ You can delete your account anytime

Full privacy policy: [link]
Questions? privacy@aquarios.com

— Aquários Team
```

#### Talking Points (For Media)
```
If contacted by journalists:

1. "Aquários is committed to user privacy from day one."
2. "We're transparent about what data we collect and why."
3. "Users control their data—they can delete anytime."
4. "We comply with LGPD and international privacy standards."
5. "False claims hurt our community. We'll address them directly."

Do NOT say:
❌ "This is political"
❌ "Activists are terrorists"
❌ "This is helping competitors"
```

community. Founded in [YEAR], Aquários prioritizes user privacy and data security.
